# Ashen Company design research

This note separates reference-game evidence from Ashen Company's intended prototype scope. The Battle Brothers and TaleWorlds posts below are primary developer material; several are historical development posts, so they support the described design lineage and should not be read as current feature roadmaps.

## Four key findings

1. **The overworld should be the main play space.** Battle Brothers describes free travel across a strategic map, with player choices about destinations, threats, contracts, hiring, supplies, and equipment. Its feature page distinguishes this strategic layer from tactical combat. Mount & Blade: Bannerlord is a useful movement reference: the player steers a party on a continuous campaign map, while other parties travel and act in the same world. For Ashen Company, use direct tap-to-set-destination travel across a compact original map; keep travel, discovery, and settlement decisions central. [Battle Brothers features](https://battlebrothersgame.com/features/) · [Battle Brothers strategic worldmap](https://battlebrothersgame.com/strategic-worldmap/) · [TaleWorlds, The Passage of Time](https://www.taleworlds.com/en/Games/Bannerlord/Blog/14)

2. **Settlements give travel a purpose.** Battle Brothers describes towns and military sites as places to hire and buy or sell equipment; the released-worldmap post notes that settlements vary in services, recruits, and goods. TaleWorlds describes Bannerlord towns as trade hubs for recruiting and quests. These support Ashen Company's settlement loop: trade, recruit, and accept delivery contracts. Delivery contracts are an Ashen Company feature choice, not a claim about a specific Battle Brothers implementation. [Battle Brothers, Dev Blog #33](https://battlebrothersgame.com/dev-blog-33-character-generation-hiring-shopping/) · [Battle Brothers, Worldmap Update Released](https://battlebrothersgame.com/worldmap-update-released/) · [TaleWorlds, Dev Blog 05/07/18](https://www.taleworlds.com/en/games/bannerlord/blog/67)

3. **Layered portraits need stable anchors and explicit draw order.** Battle Brothers' character-generation post says hair, face, and beard were separated and freely combined; armor was separated from the body and drawn over it, while smaller helmets could cover hair without oversized proportions. For Ashen Company's original bust portraits, establish a shared canvas and named anchor points for head, neck, shoulders, hands, and held gear. A practical back-to-front order is body/clothing base → neck/face → hair and beard → torso armor → hands/arms → held weapon or shield → helmet/headgear and foreground straps. Use per-layer anchor offsets and masks (for example, hair behind the helmet, face above the collar, hands in front of the torso) rather than scaling pieces until they happen to align. Treat weapon/shield ordering and these anchor details as implementation guidance for Ashen Company; the cited post specifically substantiates the hair/face/beard/body/armor separation, not this entire proposed stack. Draw only original or properly licensed assets; do not copy Battle Brothers artwork. [Battle Brothers, Dev Blog #33](https://battlebrothersgame.com/dev-blog-33-character-generation-hiring-shopping/)

4. **Offline use needs explicit install, readiness, and save-backup steps.** On iPad, Apple's current Safari instructions are Share → More → Add to Home Screen, enable “Open as Web App,” then Add. The app should first be opened online and show an explicit offline-ready state after its shell/assets are cached; then close it, enable Airplane Mode, relaunch from the Home Screen icon, and verify both launch and local save load. WebKit says website storage can be evicted under storage pressure or other conditions; default storage is best-effort, while persistent mode is only requested and granted according to heuristics. Therefore local saves must not be presented as guaranteed permanent: offer user-triggered JSON export/import and remind players to export a backup. [Apple Support: Turn a website into an app in Safari on iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-om/guide/ipad/ipad8f1f7a29/ipados) · [WebKit: Updates to Storage Policy](https://webkit.org/blog/14403/updates-to-storage-policy/)

## Scope boundary

The references support travel, settlement interaction, contracts, recruitment, trade, and layered character rendering as inspirations. Ashen Company's initial prototype target is its own live overworld, touch destination travel, settlements, original bust portraits, and offline save portability. Auto combat is a later roadmap item; these sources do not establish that it is implemented in the prototype. The feature page describes Battle Brothers' tactical-combat layer, which is useful context but does not move Ashen Company's auto-combat into P1.
