# Battle Brothers design references

## Direction

For the next feature pass, use Battle Brothers as the direct reference for both campaign and combat rules. Keep Ashen Company's compact map and current settlements, but make combat, company upkeep, progression, contract difficulty, and equipment follow Battle Brothers' documented structure instead of adding unrelated systems. The cited posts are developer descriptions from different points in development; later posts revise earlier mechanics. They establish design and UI conventions, not a complete current rulebook or exact balance values.

## Priority mapping

| Priority | Battle Brothers evidence | Ashen Company direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Combat foundation | The tactical layer uses character turns sorted by initiative, AP for movement and skills, fatigue, morale, and two armor hit zones (body and head). The early armor-as-buffer description was later updated with direct damage, so body armor, helmet armor, and hitpoints are separate values and the damage model accounts for direct damage through armor. [Tactical combat](https://battlebrothersgame.com/tactical-combat-mechanics/) · [Armor mechanics update](https://battlebrothersgame.com/dev-blog-47-progress-update-wardogs-armor-mechanics-visual-makeover/) · [Character stats](https://battlebrothersgame.com/character-stats/) | Keep body armor, helmet armor, and hitpoints as distinct combat values. Implement turn-based battles with AP-costed weapon skills; refresh AP at turn start, order allies and enemies by initiative, and let fatigue plus armor affect acting order. Keep the calculation transparent; do not copy unverified balance numbers from old posts. |
| 2. Morale and character identity | Morale checks can cause wavering and fleeing, can spread after allies flee, and are affected by kills, damage, and fallen allies. Backgrounds influence starting stats and equipment; traits add personal mechanical variation. [Tactical combat](https://battlebrothersgame.com/tactical-combat-mechanics/) · [Backgrounds and traits](https://battlebrothersgame.com/dev-blog-18-character-traits-and-backgrounds/) · [Character stats](https://battlebrothersgame.com/character-stats/) | Give recruits background-defined initial stats, gear, and short biography; add a small, legible set of traits later. Track resolve/morale separately from HP and fatigue, with visible states and simple flee/rally rules. The current named backgrounds and morale field are flavor/campaign placeholders, not this system. |
| 3. Battle aftermath and progression | The combat results screen reports damage dealt/received, kills, XP, injury time, and level gain, followed by a separate loot view. Campaign persistence carries equipment and battle-earned experience forward. Characters level and gain attributes and perks. [Combat results and loot UI](https://battlebrothersgame.com/dev-blog-31-progress-update-orcs-ui-new-weapons/) · [Persistent campaigns](https://battlebrothersgame.com/dev-blog-29-progress-update-greenlight-greenskins-persistent-campaigns/) · [Level and perk development](https://battlebrothersgame.com/dev-blog-16-progress-update-game-trailer/) | Make the battle outcome a clear bridge back to the company: casualties/injuries, individual XP and level-ups, then loot. Award recoverable enemy equipment and supplies rather than treating every defeated enemy's gear as guaranteed intact loot. Preserve surviving company, gear, and progression in the campaign save. |
| 4. Risk-readable contracts | Battle Brothers marks contracts from one to three skulls; the developer describes skulls as rough estimates, with difficulty and pay varying by rating. Contracts are offered in settlements and can respond to world situations and company renown. [Skull difficulty](https://battlebrothersgame.com/worldmap-update-0-6-0-16/) · [Contract changes](https://battlebrothersgame.com/dev-blog-61-progress-update-contracts-part-1/) · [Later contract design](https://battlebrothersgame.com/dev-blog-95-contract-changes/) | Keep the current courier and supply jobs as initial contract types, but add a visible 1–3 skull estimate and matching reward/risk before accepting. Treat skulls as guidance, not a guaranteed result. Add richer objectives and employer consequences only after encounters exist. |
| 5. Supplies, recovery, and camp | The strategic map manages crowns, food, repair tools/armor parts, ammunition, and medical supplies. World-map recovery and repair cost supplies and time. Camping lets the company recover and repair while time passes, with a chance of attracting attention. [Strategic worldmap and supplies](https://battlebrothersgame.com/strategic-worldmap/) · [Tradeable and lootable supplies](https://battlebrothersgame.com/dev-blog-38-progress-update-bandits-militia-rivers/) · [Camping](https://battlebrothersgame.com/worldmap-update-released/) | Replace the current instant camp heal with time- and inventory-based recovery. Add tools for armor repair, medical supplies for wounds, and ammo for ranged weapons when those systems are used in battle. Show shortage and daily use alongside food and wages. Camp should advance time and expose the company to a clear, restrained risk/event. |
| 6. Equipment-first UI | The character screen was designed as a central paper doll with body armor, helmet, main hand, off hand, ammunition, and accessory slots; stats, skills, passives, and status effects are listed nearby. The developers favored visible buttons and tooltips so functions were discoverable before players learned hotkeys. [Inventory and character screen](https://battlebrothersgame.com/dev-blog-10-progress-report-an-update-on-battle-brothers/) | Keep the existing equipment portrait, but make its slots and adjacent stat/skill/status panel the main point of control. Use explicit buttons, readable tooltips, and a post-battle results screen rather than hiding essential actions behind gestures. Add off-hand, ammo, and consumable slots only when corresponding rules are implemented. |

## Current prototype boundary

The dated v0.2 baseline in [verification notes](VERIFICATION.md) recorded an eight-settlement world, courier and supply contracts, markets, food and wages, recruitment, camping/foraging, equipment slots, and local save/offline support. At that baseline, people had HP and morale fields, while armor/fatigue/power were equipment values; combat-driven body/head armor, AP, initiative, morale checks, battle injuries, XP/levels, and battle loot were not implemented. This is a versioned snapshot, not a claim about later work: check the current implementation and browser evidence before describing what has shipped.

Battle Brothers art is incorporated in this prototype; provenance and the published asset terms are documented in [asset credits](ASSET-CREDITS.md). The portrait renderer aligns imported body, face, hair, armor, helmet, shield, and weapon layers. Do not describe that artwork as Ashen Company's original art.

## iPad offline note

For offline installation, open the hosted app online in Safari, add it to the Home Screen, wait for its offline-ready indicator, then close and relaunch it in Airplane Mode. Keep a JSON export in Files: cached app assets do not guarantee that iPadOS will retain local save storage. See [Apple's Add to Home Screen guide](https://support.apple.com/en-om/guide/ipad/ipad8f1f7a29/ipados) and [WebKit's storage policy notes](https://webkit.org/blog/14403/updates-to-storage-policy/).
