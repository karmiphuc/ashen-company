# Ashen Winter siege and marshal forces

Settlement sieges and marshal strongholds use twice the living company size,
including reserves, clamped to 25–50 undead. Strongholds freeze their force size
when the crisis fronts are created; sieges freeze it when the host reaches the
settlement. Road hosts retain their existing 20/24-troop rules. Occupation forces
use the siege size too, so waiting for a settlement to fall does not bypass the
large liberation battle. Casualties can reduce the actual remaining force below
the starting minimum. Repeated encounters never reroll troop identities or heal
wounds; dead marshals remain dead.

Each new liberation battle brings 10–14 allies, with at least seven militia and
three to five faction soldiers. Marshal assaults bring three to six allies,
including at least one faction soldier; an all-militia force is impossible.
Reinforcement counts and composition are seeded from the force seed, so saves
and rematches preserve the mix. Faction soldiers use existing regional faction
loadouts; militia retain existing basic gear. No races or new item families are
introduced. Ordinary rescue/assault quest allies and roaming patrol assistance
retain their existing rules.

Crisis version 4 upgrades uncommitted legacy strongholds and blocked settlements
once, preserving casualty gaps and damage. Forces already committed to a player
or NPC battle remain intact. Existing active/results battles keep their previous
allied roster; only newly started crisis battles opt into `crisisAlliesVersion: 1`.
Save validation derives allied counts from the seeded force roster and accepts
multi-digit ally IDs; it retains the old three-ally rules for legacy battles.
The deployment/save limits support 50 enemies, 14 allies and 15 fielded brothers.
