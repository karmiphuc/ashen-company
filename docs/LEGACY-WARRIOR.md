# The Frozen Vigil — awakened legacy warrior

A four-step side quest in an inherited campaign preserves a real survivor from the retired company. It supplements the sealed heirloom chain; it does not occupy an ordinary contract slot or create a newly rolled boss/race.

## Fixed selection and preservation

At retirement, rank living members by level, then melee/ranged skill and melee defense, with stable ID tie-breaking. Choose deterministically among the best three survivors within two levels of the company's highest living level, using the retired campaign seed. The next campaign seed, chosen heirloom and reloads do not reroll this choice.

Store the actual member: identity/appearance, background, level/XP, trained attributes and pending levels, learned perks, active/reserve equipment, accessories, mount, item identities/affixes and equipment condition. No player-level scaling, champion promotion, extra named rolls or hidden boss stats apply.

The new campaign cannot use the warrior before completing the chain. Thawing and preparation heal his pre-freeze HP/injuries; they do not upgrade gear or repair its wear. During the challenge, fresh injuries, HP loss, armor/shield damage, reserve changes and throwing ammunition persist after retreat. A retry cannot refill or reroll him.

Older inherited saves can recover their snapshot only from this device's validated retirement backup whose seed, day and renown match the inheritance source. Missing or unrelated backups produce no invented warrior. Existing saves without either inheritance or a frozen warrior remain unchanged.

## Four steps

| Quest | Settlement | Requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Footprints in the Chronicle | Oakwatch | Day 30, 150 renown; consume 100 crowns and 5 provisions |
| The Frozen Vigil | Ravenfell | Consume 4 wool, 5 medicine and 8 tools |
| A Warrior Remembers | Ironford | Day 60, 300 renown; consume 1,000 crowns, 6 iron and 6 tools |
| The Last Challenge | Ravenfell / Frozen Vigil | Defeat the solo warrior, collect the battle result and return to recruit him |

Turn-ins require the correct open settlement and a stationary company. The chronicle shows the preserved portrait/name/level, current objective, ready action and settlement navigation. The final challenge button travels to the reachable vigil site and starts the real encounter on arrival. Its known site is marked on the map even outside current scouting vision.

## Final encounter and reward

Exactly one enemy fights the player's normal party, without militia or reinforcements. The shared combat engine runs the preserved build, including equipment bonuses, free/learned perk effects, sets, both attachments, mount, fatigue, morale, reactions and reserve gear. This is not a power-score simulation. Ordinary enemy save restrictions are extended only for this validated warrior's identity, appearance, learned perks and second attachment.

**Player brothers can die.** The awakened warrior yields when defeated; he is recruited afterward, rather than dying as a loot source. A fleeing warrior does not complete the objective. Victory grants no gold/supply/equipment drops from him, preventing a second copy of his kit. Ordinary battle XP remains governed by the combat engine.

Recruitment preserves original level, XP, trained attributes and perks. His current gear wear/ammunition and fresh injuries remain; after a knockdown he joins with 1 HP and needs normal recovery. He occupies a normal company/formation or reserve slot and pays normal wages/food upkeep. A full roster leaves the reward pending. The final turn-in cannot recruit him twice.

## Validation

Cover fixed selection, matching-archive recovery, all four material/age gates, genuine battle actions and save/reload, one-enemy/no-allies deployment, a level-30 named build with learned perks and two attachments, retreat continuity, no duplicate loot, one-time wounded recruitment, full roster and malformed snapshots. Browser checks cover the chronicle, site navigation, challenge and saved retry on iPad. Run the existing heirloom, forge, quest battle, offline and release checks as regressions.
