# Champions and elite scaling (0.48.5)

Every eligible fighter now makes an independent seeded champion roll. The existing 1% / 3% / 6% tier chances, Bounty Hunter and temporary event bonuses apply per fighter. Starter bands remain exempt. There is no champion quota: ten or even twenty successes are allowed. Existing named elite leaders are promoted individually, and wanted contracts guarantee the first fighter while allowing other retainers to roll too. Weapon themes, independent named-item seeds, champion buffs and defeat-only trophy drops remain intact.

Top-tier camps and roaming bands scale with living company size, strongest-six average level and campaign age. Existing level/age gates still apply; tier-three veteran rank now grows to ten, while medium tier stays at three. Starter encounters stay small. Elite force size retains the normal reinforcement floor and adds a size/rank target capped at 20. A full company at rank five faces 18–20, varied by the encounter's authored base roster; smaller or less experienced companies face fewer. Reserves cannot inflate the size factor beyond 15. Higher ranks continue improving health, attack and defense. Scripted quest and Ashen Winter force sizes retain their authored rules.

New battles carry `enemyScalingVersion: 1`, accept enemy IDs 1–20 and use free walkable deployment cells when overflow would occupy a camp wall. Existing active battles keep their saved roster, statistics and legacy limits. World patrol casualty records accept forces up to 20. There is no migration of in-progress fights.

Normal loot remains capped at 24 items. Champion trophies are inserted first and may exceed that cap, up to the four named equipment slots of twenty enemies (80). Thus multiple champions cannot lose guaranteed trophies to the ordinary loot limit. Escaped champions retain their trophies. The new battle marker permits these larger result saves.

Validation covers independent rolls, ten champions in one roster, unique trophies, level/age/size gates, 18–20 elite rosters, legal placement and active reload of 15 versus 20, legacy bounds, rejection of enemy 21, persistent world casualties and forty guaranteed trophies from twenty champions. Desktop/tablet/phone browser checks confirm twenty rendered enemies and the correct troop counts without horizontal page overflow. All 685 tests pass; prepare-offline and offline browser reload cover the changed modules.
